Terms of service

GreenFuze Cloud Storage is a local connector that lets supported GreenFuze applications read game packages from a Google Drive folder selected by the user.

Permitted use

You may use the connector only with accounts, files, and software you are authorized to access. You are responsible for complying with applicable laws, software licenses, Google Drive's terms, and the terms of the client application in which the connector is used.

Account access

The connector acts only after you authorize Google Drive and select a folder. You can disconnect it through the client application or revoke authorization through your Google Account controls.

Local changes

Discovery is read-only. Installation operations download files to a local directory configured by the client application. Review prompts carefully and maintain backups of important data. The connector does not modify or delete Google Drive files.
